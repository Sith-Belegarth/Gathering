⚔  The Realm

What is Korriban?

In Belegarth, a realm is a local practice group — the place where fighters gather regularly to train, fight, and build community. Korriban is our realm, based in the Corona and Riverside area of Southern California.

Korriban fights under the banner of the Sith, a Belegarth unit whose members are bound not by geography but by shared identity. Your realm is where you practice. Your unit is whose banner you carry into battle.

Arrive curious. Leave battle-tested.

✦

⚔  Find the Flagship

Quests

When we practice locally, we meet at Promenade Park in Corona. All skill levels are welcome — veteran fighters and first-timers alike. Loaner weapons are available. Check our Facebook for the current schedule.

Beyond the field, Korriban gathers at least once a month for Craft Day or Board Game Night. Whether you make things or play things, there is always somewhere to show up.

⚔  Come Prepared

What to Bring

  • Water
  • Sunscreen
  • Padded gloves (lacrosse or hockey)
  • Kneepads (volleyball style)
  • Eye protection
  • Athletic clothes or medieval garb
  • Friends who might enjoy fighting

⚔  New to the Field?

What is Belegarth?

Belegarth Medieval Combat Society is a full-contact foam combat sport inspired by fantasy and medieval warfare. Participants build their own padded weapons and engage in organized battles, melees, and scenarios.

No experience is required. You need only the willingness to step onto the field. Loaner weapons are available at every Korriban practice.
